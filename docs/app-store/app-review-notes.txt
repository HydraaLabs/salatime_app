SalaTime provides prayer times, Quran reading and recitation, Qibla, Athkar, a Hijri calendar and prayer widgets. Its main features work without an account. On first launch, choose a language and location; location permission can be declined and a city selected manually. Notification permission is optional and is used for prayer reminders. Streaming recitations, maps, nearby mosques, account sync and AI features require an internet connection.

Optional account features: open Settings > Account. Use the email and password supplied in the App Review sign-in fields to test the account and synchronization of supported preferences and Quran/Athkar reading progress. This dedicated account is already verified and does not require a one-time email code. Sign in with Apple is also available on this screen on iOS, through Apple's native authorization dialog; the supplied demo credentials are for SalaTime email sign-in, not an Apple Account.

Account deletion: while signed in, open Settings > Account and choose Delete account. An email/password account asks for its password to confirm deletion; a social sign-in account may require a recent sign-in. Deletion removes the SalaTime account and its cloud data. Deleting the demo account is permanent; contact us if replacement access is needed.

AI features: the Classic home layout exposes AI Assistant and the app also includes an Islamic name generator. The home layout can be changed in Settings. Before either feature sends its first request, a consent dialog names 1min.ai and explains that the question or generator choices will be sent through SalaTime to that provider. Cancel sends no request. Allow saves permission on that device for both AI features. The privacy icon on each AI screen allows this permission to be withdrawn. These are generated responses, not communication with other app users; the app warns that AI can make mistakes.

No purchase or subscription is required. Public support: https://salatime.net/support . Privacy policy: https://salatime.net/privacy-policy .
